LoRaWAN®

Welcome to The Things Fundamentals on LoRaWAN! This series of chapters was created to prepare you for The Things Fundamentals certification. You’ll learn all about LoRa® and LoRaWAN and suitable use cases, and afterwards you’ll be ready to start developing your own LoRaWAN applications.
